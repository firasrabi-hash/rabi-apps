Privacy Policy

for the Rabi Kids Apps family
KidsPaint · Memory Match · Math Match · Word Builder

Short version: These apps were made by a pediatrician for children. They do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you or your child. There are no ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no in-app purchases.

No data collected No ads No tracking No account needed No in-app purchases

Who we are

These apps are created and maintained by Dr. Firas Rabi, a pediatrician, as a family of simple, ad-free learning and creativity apps for young children. This policy applies to all of the apps listed above (together, the “Apps”).

Information we collect

None. The Apps do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There is no sign-up, no login, and no account. We do not use any third-party advertising or analytics services, which is also required for apps in Apple’s Kids Category.

Information that stays on the device

Some Apps let a child type a first name or choose an age or character to personalize the experience. This information is stored only on the device using the device’s own local settings. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it never leaves the device. Deleting the app removes it.

Photos

KidsPaint can save a child’s drawing to the device’s own photo library if you choose to tap “Save.” The app only adds a picture you create; it does not read, browse, or upload your existing photos. iOS will ask your permission the first time.

Children’s privacy

The Apps are designed for children and are compliant with the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws, because we collect no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There are no links out of the app, no social features, and no user content is shared with others.

Third parties

We do not share information with third parties because we do not collect any. The Apps contain no third-party advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics SDKs.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the Apps handle information, we will update this page and change the date below.
