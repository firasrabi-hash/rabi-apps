🐝 BumbleSpell

Privacy Policy

Spelling practice for children, made by a pediatrician

Short version: BumbleSpell does not collect, send or share any information about you or your child. Everything the app keeps stays on your device.

No data collectedNo adsNo tracking No accountNo in-app purchasesWorks offline

Who we are

BumbleSpell is made by Dr. Firas Rabi, a pediatrician in Amman, Jordan, to help children practise their weekly school spelling words.

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-up, no login and no server. It makes no network requests and contains no advertising or analytics software of any kind.

What stays on the device

To work, the app saves these things only on the device, inside the app's own private storage:

  • the child's first name, if a grown-up types one (used for greetings such as "Hi, Sam!")
  • the weekly spelling words and practice results, streaks, badges and school test scores entered by a grown-up
  • rewards a grown-up sets up (for example "an ice cream after a 20-day streak")
  • optional voice recordings a grown-up makes of the words and cheers
  • an optional photo of a prize, which a grown-up picks from the photo library

None of this is ever sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Microphone and photos

The microphone is used only when a grown-up chooses to record their own voice, behind the grown-ups' lock. Recordings stay on the device. Prize photos are chosen with Apple's photo picker, which only gives the app the single photo the grown-up selects. The app cannot see the rest of the photo library.

Sharing

A grown-up can choose to share the week's word list or a badge picture, for example to a class WhatsApp group. This uses the device's standard share sheet, sits behind the grown-ups' lock, and sends only what the grown-up chooses to send. Nothing is shared automatically.

Children's privacy

BumbleSpell is made for children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws. Settings, sharing and links are behind a grown-ups' lock.

Third parties

We share nothing with third parties because we collect nothing. The app uses only Apple's own system frameworks.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
